# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
People in and around Yaoundé, mostly on mid-range Android phones over mobile data, who are
ready to pick up WhatsApp:
- **Site owners, contractors and masons** at the finishing stage, weighing up hiring an
  automatic plastering machine instead of rendering by hand.
- **Visitors and professionals** (diaspora, mission staff, business travellers) looking for a
  furnished guest-house apartment in Meyo for a few nights or a few months.
- **Men with an occasion coming up** (a wedding, the office, a ceremony) who want a garment made
  to measure, plus **young people** looking for a recognised tailoring apprenticeship.

The job they're trying to get done: decide whether SAFA is serious and real, then start a
conversation (WhatsApp, a call, or the form). Nothing is booked or bought on the site.

## Product Purpose
A bilingual (EN/FR) showcase for SAFA & Co SARL's three businesses: machine hire, guest-house
apartments, and tailoring with a training centre. Success means a visitor trusts the business
quickly, ideally by seeing the company's **own** machines, rooms and garments, and sends a message.

## Brand Personality
Precise, established, hands-on. The idea is "Le Tracé": every craft starts with a chalk line
(the mason's line on the wall, the tailor's chalk on the cloth, the line on a floor plan). The
tone is calm and confident, "by appointment" restraint rather than salesy hype. The site should
leave visitors feeling reassured by its quality.

## Anti-references
- Generic construction-company templates: hard hats, yellow-and-black stripes, stock crane
  photos.
- Booking-platform clones (Airbnb or Booking-style cards, star ratings, price tags).
- Kente or ndop patterns used as decoration, cultural-symbol borders.
- Glassmorphism, gradient blobs, icon-bento grids, fade-up on every element, preloaders.
- Anything that implies a price, "the first in Cameroon", or that stock people are SAFA staff.

## Design Principles
1. **Show the real thing.** The client's own photos and videos of machines, rooms and garments
   carry the trust. Typography frames them; it doesn't replace them.
2. **One system, three materials.** One line, one palette and one type pairing. Each division
   changes only its accent and texture.
3. **Every screen leads to a conversation.** WhatsApp is always within thumb reach.
4. **Light on the network.** Pages are static. Media is responsive, lazy and paused when off
   screen, because bills and mobile data are both real costs.
5. **Honest copy.** No prices, no unprovable claims, nothing a fact change could quietly
   falsify.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast ≥ 4.5:1, tap targets ≥ 44px, everything usable by keyboard.
`prefers-reduced-motion` gets static lines and no autoplaying video. Content stays readable
without JavaScript (the pages are prerendered). French and English are equal and complete.
